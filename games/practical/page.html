📌 Label this frame

Pause the video on the structure, then take a screenshot of the video and paste it here.
Mac: ⌘ Cmd + Ctrl + Shift + 4 → drag over the video → click here → ⌘ Cmd + V
iPad/phone: screenshot → crop → “Choose image”. Windows: Win + Shift + S → Ctrl + V.

Then: 1. tap the structure on the picture · 2. type the Latin name (suggestions from the board list) · 3. Save. Add as many pins as you want.
